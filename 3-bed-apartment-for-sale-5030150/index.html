Description

Modern elegance meets ultimate convenience at 52 The Oval, proudly presented for sale by Casey Kennedy Estate Agents.This exceptional first floor apartment offers a rare walk-in experience. Having undergone an extensive, top-to-bottom renovation between 2023 and 2024, every corner of this home exudes contemporary style and premium comfort.

Tucked away in the superbly located Tullyvale development, adjacent to the Cherrywood LUAS stop, Cherrywood Business Park, new Cherrywood Town Centre with a planned 30,000m2+ of retail leisure and community spaces to rival Dundrum, and wide open spaces in Tully Park and Druids Valley from where there are stunning views across to Killiney Hill.

Step inside to discover a bright, beautifully curated living space featuring brand-new flooring, sleek new internal doors, new storage options, and a flawless paint finish. The heart of the home is an open-plan space filled with natural light from the floor to ceiling glazing opening to the spacious balcony, giving plenty of space for al-fresco dining. A seat in the dining area box window helps utilize this space, providing useful additional storage The new fitted kitchen comes equipped with excellent appliances - an electric induction hob, integrated eye-level oven, microwave, fridge-freezer and dishwasher.

Both bathrooms have been completely stripped and beautifully renovated with high-quality fixtures. A new water pump installed in 2023 ensures excellent water pressure, while an upgraded high efficiency gas boiler ensures this property qualifies for a lower rate Green mortgage.

The three bedrooms are generous double rooms. The main bedroom has a dramatic westerly facing window 8ft long. It also has a study/dressing area, fitted wardrobes and an ensuite bathroom. The second bedroom is 5.6m long with two windows and a lovely bright study/dressing area with fitted wardrobes. The middle bedroom is a good sized double, also with a fitted wardrobe.

The entrance hall has plenty of space for coats and shoes. From here door leads to the main bathroom which doubles as a utility room equipped with a washing machine and dryer, both of which are included in the sale.

The meticulously landscaped communal grounds are currently benefiting from an outdoor lighting upgrade. To the east you get a wonderful view of Killiney Hill from the end of the balcony or from the walkway at the rear of The Oval beside Druid Valley Park.

Additional parking spaces (clearly marked FOR SALE) are available to purchase from the management company.The apartment includes one designated parking space in the secure, gated underground car park, with lift access bringing you directly to your apartment level..Thee is very restricted visitor parking with active clamping.When viewing we suggest you park in the clearly signposted "Best Car Parks" parking garage beside the Cherrywood LUAS stop, less than 5 minutes walk from the apartment.

The development features controlled building access and comprehensive CCTV monitoring for maximum security. There is an on-site concierge. The annual service charge of 2,900 includes bins and weekly cleaning of the communal areas.

Location is everything, and 52 The Oval delivers effortlessly. Within the development you have a Leisure Centre with swimming pool and a Park Academy creche. You are just a 5-minute walk from the Green Line Luas, offering swift and direct access straight into Dublin City Centre. Right on your doorstep, a vibrant hub of local amenities awaits within a 5-minute stroll, including a SPAR, Tesco Express, Starbucks, Zambrero, Ben Dunne Gym, TRIB3 gym, and the Laya Health & Wellbeing Clinic. The new Cherrywood Town Centre on 8.65 acres with 30,000 square meters of high-density retail, leisure, and community spaces (to rival Dundrum) is currently under construction - (the basement parking has been built and the site is currently advertised for sale). The plans include a sports arena suitable for Ice Hockey.and many other sports and events. Nearby the extensive Tully Park includes an ancient church ruin, stone crosses and a children`s playground. This accesses the Dublin Mountains Way which passes over Leadmines Hill and on through the Dublin Mountains.

This is a beautifully maintained and extensively upgraded owner-occupied apartment which is not rent capped.The sellers are relocating overseas, so there is no onward chain. Their closing timeline is flexible to meet your requirements - a quick sale can be facilitated if needed. It comes fully equipped with floor coverings, window coverings,and the full range of appliances. Furniture is negotiable with the buyer.

Early viewing of this exceptional apartment is highly recommended.
